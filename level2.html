Level 2: Typing Commands!

Step 1 of 4

⌨️ From Keys to Commands

In Level 1, you moved your animal by pressing arrow keys. That works for a game, but real programmers give instructions by typing commands.

When you type commands, you can write down a whole plan first, and then the computer follows it all at once!

Step 2 of 4

Your Four Commands

up() down() left() right()
  • The word says which way to move.
  • The ( ) parentheses tell the computer "do it now!" Every command needs them.
  • Each command moves your animal one square.

Step 3 of 4

🧐 Computers Are Picky!

A person can guess what you mean if you make a typo. A computer can't! It needs every command spelled exactly right.

right() ✅ works! rigth() ❌ misspelled right ❌ missing ( )

Don't worry if you make a mistake. The computer will tell you which line to fix.

Step 4 of 4

📜 Writing a Program

Put each command on its own line. The computer runs them from top to bottom, one at a time:

right() right() down()

This moves your animal right, right again, then down. A list of commands like this is a program!
