Level 1: What Is Coding?

Step 1 of 4

💻 Talking to Computers

Coding is the language we use to talk to computers and tell them what to do.

A computer doesn't understand English sentences like "How's it going today?" or "Could you please clean my shoes?" Instead, we use coding languages with very specific commands that a computer can understand.

Step 2 of 4

🤖 Computers Follow Instructions Exactly

Imagine telling a robot to "make a sandwich." It wouldn't know where to start! You would have to give it every single step:

1. Get two slices of bread 2. Open the peanut butter 3. Spread peanut butter on one slice 4. Put the slices together

Computers are the same. They do exactly what you tell them, one step at a time, in order. A list of steps like this is called a program.

Step 3 of 4

⬆️ Your First Commands

In this level, the arrow keys on your keyboard are your commands! Each press tells your animal to move one space.

  • ↑ moves up one space
  • ↓ moves down one space
  • ← moves left one space
  • → moves right one space

Want to go 3 spaces to the right? Press → three times. One instruction for each step!
