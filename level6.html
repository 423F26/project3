Level 6: Debugging!

Step 1 of 4

🐛 What Is a Bug?

A bug is a mistake in a program that makes it do the wrong thing, or not work at all.

Fun fact: in 1947, computer scientist Grace Hopper's team found a real moth stuck inside their computer! They taped it in their notebook and wrote "first actual case of bug being found."

Finding and fixing mistakes in code is called debugging.

Step 2 of 4

Every Programmer Makes Bugs!

Even the best programmers in the world write bugs every day. A bug doesn't mean you're bad at coding. Fixing bugs is a normal part of the job!

There are two main kinds of bugs:

  • Typing bugs: a misspelled word or a missing symbol. The computer stops and tells you something is wrong.
  • Thinking bugs: the code runs, but it does the wrong thing. These are sneakier!

Step 3 of 4

🔍 How to Squash a Bug

  • 1. Read the message. It usually tells you which line has the problem.
  • 2. Check that line carefully. Look at the spelling, the ( ), and the { }.
  • 3. Watch it run. Where does your animal go wrong?
  • 4. Fix one thing, then run it again.
